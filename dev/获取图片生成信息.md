## GET 获取图片生成信息

GET /unified/ai/openApi/image/get

获取图片生成信息

### 请求参数

|名称|位置|类型|必选|说明|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|id|query|string| 是 |none|
|Authorization|header|string| 否 |none|
|clientid|header|string| 否 |none|
|Open-Api-Token|header|string| 否 |none|

> 返回示例

> 200 Response

```
{"code":0,"msg":"string","data":{"id":0,"userId":0,"imageTaskId":"string","model":"string","contentText":"string","contentImageUrl":"string","imagesUrl":"string","resolution":"string","completionTokens":0,"status":null,"failReason":"string","createTime":"2019-08-24T14:15:22Z","updateTime":"2019-08-24T14:15:22Z","contentImageOssId":"string","imageOssIds":"string","size":"string","quality":"string","maskOssId":"string","maskOssUrl":"string","editMode":"string","background":"string"}}
```
